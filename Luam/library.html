04 Shelf

Explained in 60 seconds

One minute each, written by Luam in plain words.

iGood to know. These summaries are general and simplified. They cannot tell you what you have. Only a qualified professional can do that.

  • 60s

    Anxiety

    Attunement

    Anxiety is your alarm system firing early. A fast heart and tight chest are the body preparing to act. It becomes a problem when the alarm rings often with no real danger, or gets in the way of daily life. Slow breathing, regular sleep and talking to someone help, and so does therapy.

    WHO fact sheet ↗ (opens in a new tab)
  • 60s

    Depression

    Vitality

    Depression is more than a sad day. It is weeks of low mood or loss of interest, often with changes in sleep, appetite and energy. It is common and treatable. If this sounds like you, speak to a clinician or someone you trust.

    WHO fact sheet ↗ (opens in a new tab)
  • 60s

    Stress

    Attunement

    Stress is the body responding to demand. Short bursts can help you perform. Long, unrelenting stress wears down sleep, mood and health. Rest and support are part of the answer, not a reward.

    WHO Q&A ↗ (opens in a new tab)
  • 60s

    Burnout

    Vitality

    Burnout is exhaustion from long-term work stress, with distance from the job and less ability to do it well. The fix is rarely just a day off. It usually means changing workload, support or boundaries.

    WHO statement ↗ (opens in a new tab)
  • 60s

    Grief

    Connection

    Grief is love with nowhere to go. It comes in waves, not stages in a neat order, and there is no correct timeline. Being with people, routines and remembering all help. If it stays frozen for a long time, ask for help.

  • 60s

    Therapy

    Purpose

    Therapy is a structured conversation with a trained professional. It is not only for crisis. Methods such as CBT teach practical skills, and the relationship with your therapist matters a great deal. It is fine to try more than one therapist.

  • 60s

    Sleep and mood

    Vitality

    Poor sleep makes feelings louder and thinking slower, and low mood can also disturb sleep. Regular times, daylight, less late caffeine and a screen-free wind-down help many people.

  • 60s

    Panic attacks

    Attunement

    A panic attack is a surge of fear with a racing heart, breathlessness and a sense of doom. It feels dangerous and is not, and it peaks and passes, often within minutes. Slow out-breaths help. Chest pain is worth checking with a clinician.

    Hurry has no blessing.

    Rushing past rest or feelings costs more later.

    We cope better together than alone.
