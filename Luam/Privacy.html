Legal

How we handle your information.

What the website counts, what stays on your device, how we treat health information, and your rights under Kenyan law.

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short

  • The public website sets no cookies. It counts anonymous visits and button taps. It never records your name, your IP address or anything you type.
  • Your health information is kept apart from the website. It starts only when you book or use a clinical service.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and you can complain to the Data Protection Commissioner.

Who we are

Luam handles any collected information according to KDPA

Questions about this policy, or requests about your information, go to hello@luam.co.ke

Two parts, two sets of rules

We keep the public website and your care separate so that browsing never touches your health information.

  • The public website (this site, the blog, the library, events and the check-in): no accounts, no cookies, no personal details collected.
  • Booking and care: when you book, message us or see a clinician, we handle your details and health information under the rules for sensitive personal data in sections 6 to 9.
  • Symmetry, our reflection tool: what you write stays on your device unless a feature clearly says otherwise.

We do not store your IP address, your name, anything you type, or any identifier that follows you between visits. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we count nothing.

What stays on your device

Some features remember things in your browser so they work properly. This information is not sent to us.

  • Your language choice and which check-in tips you have tried.
  • Booking drafts: the name, contact, date and time you type into the booking form are saved in your browser so you can continue later. They are sent only when you choose to send your booking by WhatsApp.
  • Symmetry: mood notes and journal entries are saved in your browser.

On a shared or public phone or computer, clear your browser data after using the booking form or Symmetry, because the next person could see what you saved.

When you book or contact us

If you book through WhatsApp, call us or write to us, we receive what you send: usually your name, contact details, preferred time and anything you choose to say. We use it only to arrange and provide care, and to answer you. WhatsApp is run by Meta and handles the message on its way to us under its own privacy terms.

Please do not send detailed medical history by message. Save it for your session.

Health information

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, information about your health is sensitive personal data. We process it only to provide your care, with your consent or where the law allows, and we handle it under the professional secrecy that binds our clinicians.

We collect only what is needed:

  • Identification: your name and contact details.
  • Clinical history: notes and session records kept by your clinician to plan your treatment.
  • App performance data: anonymous measures of how Symmetry runs, kept apart from your medical file.

We never use your health information for profiling, targeted marketing or advertising.

Where data is stored

Clinical records are stored with your clinician and released or transferred only at your request.

The anonymous website counts, and the pages themselves, are processed by providers whose servers may be outside Kenya. These contain no health information. If we ever need to send personal data outside Kenya, we will do so only as the Data Protection Act allows and tell you first.

Security and retention

Information is encrypted while it travels between your device and our systems, and clinical records are encrypted where they are stored Only the people who need a record to give you care can open it. No system is completely risk free. If a breach puts your information at risk, we will tell the Data Protection Commissioner and the people affected within the time the law requires.

We keep clinical records for as long as the law on medical records requires, and then archive or destroy them safely.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may:

  • be told how your information is used;
  • ask for a copy of your information;
  • ask us to correct anything wrong or incomplete;
  • object to some uses, or withdraw consent you gave;
  • ask us to delete information we no longer need;
  • ask for your information in a form you can move elsewhere.

Some clinical records must be kept by law, so we may not be able to delete them. We will explain if that applies. To use any right, write to hello@luam.co.ke. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Children and young people

The public website collects no personal information from anyone. For care, a parent or guardian must consent for a person under 18, and we involve the young person in decisions as much as their age and understanding allow.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we change the date at the top. If the change affects how we use health information, we will tell you before it applies to you.
