Conditions we treat in Nairobi: depression, anxiety, trauma and more.

It's okay to ask for help with any of this. People come to us at very different stages — some in crisis, some after months of feeling unlike themselves. Both are good reasons to book a first appointment.

Depression

Persistent low mood, loss of interest, or exhaustion that won't lift.

Anxiety

Constant worry, panic attacks, or a racing mind you can't switch off.

Trauma & PTSD

Common questions about seeing a psychiatrist or psychologist.

Is everything I say confidential?+

Yes. What you discuss with your clinician stays between you, in line with professional and legal confidentiality standards — with the only exceptions being situations where there's a risk to your safety or someone else's, which your clinician will always explain clearly.

What actually happens in the first session?+

Your clinician will ask about what's brought you in, your history, and what support looks like for you. There's no obligation to have the "right words" — it's a conversation, not a test.

Can I have sessions by video instead of in person?+

Yes, most of our clinicians offer video consultations, and many patients continue their entire course of care this way.

Do I need a referral to book?+

No referral is needed. You can book directly, whether this is your first time seeking care or you're switching providers.

What if I'm not sure whether I need a psychiatrist or a psychologist?+

That's a normal thing to be unsure about. Tell us what you're experiencing when you book, or call us, and we'll match you with the right clinician.
