What this website is, and is not.

Our content informs. It does not diagnose or treat, and it is not a crisis service.

Last updated 23 November 2026

In an emergency, call 999 or 112. Mental health hotlines are on our Emergency page.

In short

  • Everything on this site is for information and education, not for diagnosis or treatment.
  • Our tools support reflection. They do not replace a clinician.
  • Luam is not a crisis service.
  • Results differ from person to person.

Information, not treatment

Articles, the library, workshops, events, the check-in and conceptual frameworks such as Brain Gardening and Niksen are written for information and education. They are not a substitute for professional medical, psychiatric or psychological diagnosis, advice or treatment. Do not delay seeking care, or stop or change any medicine, because of something you read here.

Self-reflection tools

The check-in, Symmetry and cognitive exercises help you notice and think about your own patterns. They are not tests or assessments, and what they show is not a diagnosis. If something worries you, speak to a clinician.

Scheduled care, not a crisis service

Luam works with licensed psychologists and psychiatrists, but our website, apps and booking are for scheduled care. We do not offer on-demand crisis response, emergency psychiatric admission or dispatch. In an emergency, call 999 or 112 or go to the nearest emergency facility.

No clinician and patient relationship from the site

Using this website, sending us a message or reading a clinician's profile does not create a clinician and patient relationship. That begins only when you complete intake and have a session. See our Terms.

Images and illustrations

The artwork on this site, including trees, skies, silhouettes and images of calm people, is decorative and symbolic. It does not show patients or clients and it is not a literal picture of clinical outcomes. Photographs on our therapy page are of the practitioners they name.

Results differ

Each person's circumstances, history and effort are different. Our methods rest on established behavioural science, but outcomes will vary. Research we cite describes groups of people. It does not guarantee a result for you.

Emergency directory and outside links

Contacts and opening hours on the Emergency page come from public sources for convenience. We try to keep them current but cannot guarantee that an organisation is open, staffed or has space. Confirm before you travel. We do not control other websites we link to.

Accuracy and currency

Fees, availability, laws and regulations can change. We aim to keep this site current. Where something here conflicts with what a clinician or regulator tells you, follow the clinician or regulator. To report an error, write to hello@luam.co.ke.
