The terms for using Luam.

How the website and our care services work, what we ask of you, and what you can expect from us.

Last updated 23 November 2026

In an emergency, do not use this website. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call 999 or 112, or go to the nearest emergency facility. Hotlines are on our Emergency page.

In short

  • The website gives information. Care begins only when you book and have a session with a licensed clinician.
  • Sessions are KES 3,000, paid at the clinic. Sliding-scale support is available.
  • What you tell your clinician is confidential, with a few exceptions the law sets.
  • We cannot promise any particular result.

Using this website

By using luam.co.ke you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the site. You must be 18 or older to book care for yourself. A parent or guardian books care for someone younger.

Articles, the library, the check-in and other tools on the site are general information. They are not medical, psychological or psychiatric advice, and they do not diagnose or treat anything.

When care begins

A formal clinician and patient relationship starts only when you complete our intake and begin a session with one of our licensed psychologists or psychiatrists after signing a consent form. Until then, nothing on this site creates that relationship.

Booking, fees and cancelling

  • A session costs KES 3,000, in person or by video, paid at the clinic. Ask us about sliding-scale support when you book.
  • If you use medical insurance, ask us whether it can apply to your visit.

    • Video sessions

    Video sessions are for people who are physically in Kenya at the time of the session. Some things cannot be done safely online, such as prescribing controlled substances or managing an acute crisis. Your clinician will tell you when you need to be seen in person. Please use a private place and a secure connection.

    Confidentiality and its limits

    What you tell your clinician is confidential. A clinician may share information without your consent only where the law requires or allows it. The usual cases are a serious risk to your life or someone else's, protecting a child from harm, and a court order. Your clinician will explain this at your first session.

    How we handle your data is set out in our Privacy policy.

    Emergencies

    Luam and Symmetry are not crisis services. If you or someone you are with is in danger of self-harm, has experienced severe trauma, or needs urgent medical care, do not wait for an appointment. Call 999 or 112, or go to the nearest emergency facility. National and mental health hotlines are on the Emergency page.

    Licensing of our clinicians

    Clinical care is given only by practitioners registered with the right Kenyan regulator: psychiatrists with the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC), and psychologists with the Counsellors and Psychologists Board. They work within the scope of practice Kenyan law sets, and you may check any clinician's registration with the regulator.

    No guarantee of outcomes

    People respond to care differently. We cannot promise a particular result or timeline. Do not stop or change medicine because of something you read on this site. Speak to your prescriber first.

    Other organisations and links

    The Emergency page and some articles list independent organisations and websites. We do not run or control them and cannot promise their availability, accuracy or quality of care. We try to keep the list current, but it can go out of date. Please confirm details before relying on them.
