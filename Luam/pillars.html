Connection

Isolation is among the strongest risk factors for poor mental health. Trusted relationships are among the strongest protections. This pillar covers family, friends, faith and community.

One name Decide who you would call on your worst night. Tell them.
One table Share a meal each week with people who are glad to see you.
One ask Say what you need while it is still small.

Vitality

The brain runs on the body. Short sleep, a still body and an empty stomach make every other pillar harder to hold up.

7–9
Hours of sleep a night for most adults
150
Minutes of movement a week, the WHO minimum for adults
Daily
Regular meals and water, with less alcohol and tobacco

Attunement

Attunement is noticing your own state early enough to do something about it. It is the skill underneath stress management, and the first move in CBT.

Notice Find where it sits in the body. A tight jaw, shallow breath, a heavy chest.
Name Put one word on the feeling. Labelling an emotion tends to lower its intensity.
Adjust Breathe out longer than you breathe in, take a walk, or drop one task.

Purpose

Purpose is a direction to walk in. It needs something worth getting up for, work you can do well, some control over your days, and enough income to plan ahead.

One line Finish this sentence in writing: I am here to…
One step Pick the smallest action toward it that you can take this week.
One measure Decide how you will know it moved.
