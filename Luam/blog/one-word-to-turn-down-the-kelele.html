You are more than the name you give yourself

A client asks you to explain your work. Someone invites you to contribute in a meeting. You pause, then hear the familiar explanation: I’m not a confident person. It sounds settled, so you hold back.

Later, perhaps after a difficult job search, a slow week in business or a tense conversation at home, another label appears: I have a temper. That’s just me. It may describe something real. It may also be doing more work than it should.

We all use shorthand to describe ourselves. The trouble is when a description becomes a boundary — when “I sometimes feel nervous” quietly turns into “I cannot do this”. You can acknowledge a pattern without making it your whole identity.

A description is not a life sentence

Calling yourself anxious, shy or bad with money may feel like an efficient explanation. But a label can make you notice only the moments that confirm it. The times you manage something differently can pass without much attention.

Researchers have explored what happens when people learn that personality can change. In a study involving more than 1,600 students in grades 8 to 10, Yeager and colleagues (2013) found that students taught about the brain’s capacity to change were less likely to interpret an offence as deliberate and less interested in revenge. The difference was still visible eight months later.

A smaller trial by Schleider and Weisz (2016), involving 96 young people aged 12 to 15 who were experiencing or at risk of anxiety or depression, found that a single 30-minute online session about personality change helped participants feel more in control and recover faster from a stressful task than a supportive session. The study did not establish whether the effect lasted.

Change can be gradual

Adults are not fixed in place either. A review led by Brent Roberts examined 207 studies involving more than 20,000 people. Following interventions such as therapy, personality scores shifted, particularly in emotional stability. In studies that followed up later, those gains mostly held.

Personality is generally steadier than a passing mood. Still, steady is not the same as unchangeable. Much of the mindset research involved teenagers in the United States, so it is best read as an encouraging possibility, not a promise that change is effortless.

A small reflection · two minutes

Think of one label you often use for yourself. There is no need to argue with it. Just make it a little more precise.

  1. Notice the label. Complete the sentence: I am ___. Perhaps it is shy, impatient, disorganised or bad with money.
  2. Describe the situation. Try: I tend to go quiet when I’m tired and the room is noisy.
  3. Leave a little room. Add: One thing I’m practising is speaking once, even when I feel nervous.

You do not have to replace every difficult label with a positive one. An accurate description is already a kinder place to begin.

Let it become a habit

When the old label turns up, try giving it a little more context:

If–then planIf I hear myself say “I’m just a ___ person”, then I’ll ask, “When does this tend to happen?”

This is not about pretending everything is fine. It is about describing yourself accurately enough to notice where you still have choices.

When a little support would help

Reframing a thought cannot solve everything, and you do not have to handle everything alone. If harsh labels such as “I’m worthless” or “I’m a failure” keep returning, feel completely true, or come with low mood for weeks, consider speaking with a licensed psychologist. These thoughts can occur with depression or anxiety, both of which can be treated.

If you are in immediate danger or thinking of ending your life, call 999 or 112 in Kenya, or open the Emergency page.

Wewe ni zaidi ya jina unalojiita

Mteja anakuomba ueleze kazi yako. Mtu anakualika uchangie kwenye mkutano. Unasita, kisha ile kauli ya kawaida inajitokeza: Mimi si mtu wa kujiamini. Inaonekana kama jibu kamili, kwa hiyo unajizuia kuchangia.

Baadaye, labda baada ya kutafuta kazi bila mafanikio, wiki ya biashara kuwa tulivu au mazungumzo magumu nyumbani, unajiambia: Mimi nina hasira. Ndivyo nilivyo. Huenda kauli hiyo inaeleza jambo la kweli. Lakini si lazima iwe hadithi nzima.

Sote hutumia maneno mafupi kujieleza. Tatizo huanza maelezo yanapogeuka kuwa mipaka — “wakati mwingine huwa na wasiwasi” inakuwa “siwezi kufanya hili”. Unaweza kutambua tabia fulani bila kuifanya iwe utambulisho wako wote.

Maelezo si hukumu ya maisha

Kujita mtu mwenye wasiwasi, mwenye haya au asiyejua kutumia pesa kunaweza kuonekana kama njia rahisi ya kujieleza. Lakini lebo hiyo inaweza kukufanya uone zaidi nyakati zinazoiunga mkono. Nyakati unapofanya mambo kwa namna tofauti zinaweza kupita bila kuzitambua.

Watafiti wamechunguza kinachotokea watu wanapojifunza kuwa haiba inaweza kubadilika. Katika utafiti wa wanafunzi zaidi ya 1,600 wa madarasa ya 8 hadi 10, Yeager na wenzake (2013) waliona kuwa wanafunzi waliofundishwa kuhusu uwezo wa ubongo kubadilika hawakuwa wepesi kuona kosa kama lililofanywa kwa makusudi, wala kutamani kulipiza kisasi. Tofauti hiyo ilionekana hata miezi minane baadaye.

Jaribio dogo la Schleider na Weisz (2016), lililohusisha vijana 96 wenye umri wa miaka 12 hadi 15 waliokuwa na wasiwasi au huzuni, au walio katika hatari ya hali hizo, lilionyesha kuwa kipindi kimoja cha mtandaoni cha dakika 30 kuhusu uwezekano wa haiba kubadilika kiliwasaidia washiriki kujihisi kuwa na uwezo zaidi wa kuchukua hatua na kupata nafuu haraka baada ya zoezi lenye msongo. Utafiti huo haukuonyesha kama athari hiyo iliendelea kwa muda mrefu.

Mabadiliko yanaweza kuwa ya taratibu

Watu wazima pia hawajafungwa mahali pamoja. Mapitio yaliyoongozwa na Brent Roberts yalichunguza tafiti 207 zilizohusisha zaidi ya watu 20,000. Baada ya hatua kama tiba ya kisaikolojia, vipimo vya haiba vilibadilika, hasa katika utulivu wa kihisia. Katika tafiti zilizofuatilia washiriki baadaye, mabadiliko hayo mengi yaliendelea.

Haiba huwa thabiti zaidi kuliko hisia za muda mfupi. Lakini kuwa thabiti si sawa na kutobadilika. Tafiti nyingi za mtazamo huu zilihusisha vijana nchini Marekani; kwa hiyo zichukulie kama ishara ya matumaini, si ahadi kwamba mabadiliko hayahitaji juhudi.

Tafakari fupi · dakika mbili

Fikiria lebo moja unayotumia mara nyingi kujieleza. Huhitaji kuibishia. Ieleze tu kwa usahihi zaidi.

  1. Itambue. Kamilisha sentensi: Mimi ni ___. Labda ni mwenye haya, mwepesi wa kukasirika, asiyejipanga au asiyejua kutumia pesa.
  2. Eleza hali. Jaribu: Huwa nanyamaza ninapochoka na mahali pana kelele.
  3. Acha nafasi kidogo. Ongeza: Jambo moja ninalojizoeza ni kuchangia angalau mara moja, hata ninapokuwa na wasiwasi.

Huhitaji kubadilisha kila lebo ngumu na kauli chanya. Kujieleza kwa usahihi ni mwanzo mzuri.

Ifanye iwe desturi

Lebo ya zamani inaporudi, ipe muktadha kidogo:

Mpango wa ikiwa-basiNikijikuta nikisema “mimi ni mtu wa ___ tu”, basi nitajiuliza, “Huwa inatokea katika hali gani?”

Hii si kujifanya kila kitu kiko sawa. Ni kujieleza kwa usahihi ili kuona mahali ambapo bado una nafasi ya kuchagua.

Wakati msaada unaweza kusaidia

Kubadilisha namna unavyofikiria hakuwezi kutatua kila jambo, na si lazima uyakabili yote peke yako. Ikiwa mawazo makali kama “sina thamani” au “mimi ni mtu wa kushindwa” yanajirudia, yanaonekana kuwa ukweli usiopingika, au yanaambatana na hali ya huzuni kwa wiki kadhaa, fikiria kuzungumza na mwanasaikolojia aliyeidhinishwa. Mawazo haya yanaweza kuhusiana na sonona au wasiwasi; hali zote mbili zinaweza kutibiwa.

Ukiwa katika hatari ya haraka au ukifikiria kujidhuru, piga 999 au 112 nchini Kenya, au fungua ukurasa wa Dharura.
