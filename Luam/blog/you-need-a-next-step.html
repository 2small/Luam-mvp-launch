You do not need the whole plan to begin

You have a plan open in front of you. Perhaps it is a CV to send, a client to follow up, stock to source, a course to finish, or a project waiting at work. You have adjusted the plan more than once. The first step is still waiting.

It is easy to read that as a lack of discipline. Sometimes it is simply that planning has become more comfortable than beginning. The plan keeps getting polished; the work remains politely untouched.

You do not need to solve the whole project tonight. A small action, attached to a time and place, can be enough to get things moving.

Why imagining progress can feel like progress

Thinking about a good outcome can be pleasant. In four experiments, Kappes and Oettingen (2011) found that people who pictured their desired future in very positive terms showed less energy than those who thought about it more realistically. In one study, students who imagined the coming week going brilliantly reported getting less done the following week. These were small studies, so treat the finding as something to be aware of, not a rule for everyone.

The answer is not to stop planning. It is to bring the plan closer to the next real step. A review of 94 studies involving more than 8,000 participants (Gollwitzer and Sheeran, 2006) found that “if–then” plans had a medium-to-large average effect on goal achievement. A plan such as “After breakfast, I’ll send one application” or “When I close the shop, I’ll check tomorrow’s stock” makes the next move easier to recognise.

Let the first step be small

We often wait for motivation to arrive before we begin. Sometimes it appears after we have started. In the COBRA trial, 440 adults with depression received either behavioural activation — scheduling small, meaningful activities — or cognitive behavioural therapy. After 12 months, the groups had the same average score on a standard depression questionnaire. The researchers concluded that behavioural activation was not inferior to CBT in this trial.

This does not mean that difficulty starting is a sign of depression. It simply offers a useful idea: make the first move small enough to do without waiting for a perfect mood.

A gentle start · two minutes

Think of something you have been meaning to move forward: a job search, a side business, a work task, a personal project or a course. Choose just the first visible action.

  1. Make it concrete. Open the CV. Send one message to a potential client. List the stock you need. Read one page. Write the first sentence. Keep it small enough to begin.
  2. Give it a place. For example: After breakfast, before opening WhatsApp; or after closing the shop, while the day is still fresh.
  3. Begin for two minutes. You can stop when the time is up. Starting is all you promised yourself.

A small beginning is not a smaller ambition. It is simply a way into the work.

Make the next step easier to find

Attach the action to something that already happens in your day:

If–then planIf I finish breakfast, then I’ll spend two minutes sending one application before I open social media. Or: if I close the shop, then I’ll note tomorrow’s first task before heading home.

Once a week, give the larger plan ten quiet minutes. Let what you actually manage to do shape the next version. Planning should support the work, not use up all the energy meant for it.

When getting started feels unusually difficult

If even small tasks have felt out of reach for more than two weeks, or you have been feeling hopeless or unusually flat, consider speaking with a licensed psychologist or doctor. Difficulty starting can have many causes; you do not need to work them out alone.

If you are in immediate danger or thinking of ending your life, call 999 or 112 in Kenya, or open the Emergency page.

Huhitaji mpango mzima ili uanze

Una mpango mbele yako. Labda ni CV ya kutuma, mteja wa kumfuatilia, bidhaa za kutafuta, kozi ya kumaliza au kazi inayokusubiri ofisini. Umeubadilisha mpango huo mara kadhaa. Hatua ya kwanza bado inakusubiri.

Ni rahisi kudhani kwamba huna nidhamu. Wakati mwingine, kupanga tu kumekuwa rahisi kuliko kuanza. Mpango unaendelea kung’arishwa; kazi yenyewe bado haijaguswa.

Huhitaji kutatua mradi mzima leo. Hatua ndogo yenye muda na mahali maalumu inaweza kutosha kuanzisha mwendo.

Kuwazia maendeleo kunaweza kuhisi kama kuyafanya

Kufikiria matokeo mazuri kunaweza kufurahisha. Katika majaribio manne, Kappes na Oettingen (2011) waliona kuwa watu waliowazia maisha yao ya baadaye kwa matumaini makubwa sana walionyesha nguvu ndogo ya kuchukua hatua kuliko wale waliofikiria hali kwa uhalisia zaidi. Katika utafiti mmoja, wanafunzi waliojiwazia wiki ijayo ikiwa nzuri sana waliripoti kufanya kazi kidogo zaidi wiki iliyofuata. Tafiti hizi zilikuwa ndogo; zichukulie kama jambo la kuzingatia, si kanuni inayomhusu kila mtu.

Jibu si kuacha kupanga. Ni kuusogeza mpango karibu na hatua halisi inayofuata. Mapitio ya tafiti 94 zilizohusisha zaidi ya washiriki 8,000 (Gollwitzer na Sheeran, 2006) yaliona kuwa mipango ya “ikiwa-basi” ilikuwa na athari ya wastani hadi kubwa katika kufikia malengo. Mpango kama “Baada ya chai ya asubuhi, nitatuma ombi moja la kazi” au “Nikifunga biashara, nitakagua bidhaa za kesho” hufanya hatua inayofuata iwe rahisi kuitambua.

Ruhusu hatua ya kwanza iwe ndogo

Mara nyingi tunasubiri motisha kabla ya kuanza. Wakati mwingine motisha hujitokeza baada ya kuanza. Katika jaribio la COBRA, watu wazima 440 wenye sonona walipata ama tiba ya uanzishaji wa tabia — kupanga shughuli ndogo zenye maana — au tiba ya utambuzi na tabia. Baada ya miezi 12, makundi hayo yalikuwa na wastani sawa katika kipimo cha kawaida cha sonona. Watafiti walihitimisha kuwa tiba ya uanzishaji wa tabia haikuwa duni kuliko CBT katika jaribio hilo.

Hii haimaanishi kuwa kushindwa kuanza ni ishara ya sonona. Inatoa tu wazo rahisi: fanya hatua ya kwanza iwe ndogo kiasi cha kuanza bila kusubiri hali kamili ya moyo.

Mwanzo wa taratibu · dakika mbili

Fikiria jambo ambalo umekuwa ukitaka kulisogeza mbele: kutafuta kazi, biashara ndogo, jukumu kazini, mradi binafsi au kozi. Chagua hatua ya kwanza tu inayoonekana.

  1. Ifanye iwe wazi. Fungua CV. Mtumie mteja mtarajiwa ujumbe mmoja. Orodhesha bidhaa unazohitaji. Soma ukurasa mmoja. Andika sentensi ya kwanza. Iwe ndogo kiasi cha kuanza.
  2. Ipangie mahali. Kwa mfano: Baada ya chai ya asubuhi, kabla ya kufungua WhatsApp; au baada ya kufunga biashara, kabla hujaondoka.
  3. Anza kwa dakika mbili. Unaweza kusimama muda ukiisha. Ahadi yako ilikuwa kuanza tu.

Mwanzo mdogo haupunguzi ukubwa wa ndoto. Unakupa tu njia ya kuifikia.

Rahisisha kuiona hatua inayofuata

Unganisha hatua hiyo na jambo ambalo tayari hutokea katika siku yako:

Mpango wa ikiwa-basiNikimaliza chai ya asubuhi, basi nitatumia dakika mbili kutuma ombi moja la kazi kabla ya kufungua mitandao ya kijamii. Au: nikifunga biashara, nitaandika kazi ya kwanza ya kesho kabla ya kuondoka.

Mara moja kwa wiki, jipe dakika kumi za kutazama mpango mkubwa. Ruhusu kile ulichoweza kufanya kiuelekeze toleo linalofuata. Kupanga kunapaswa kusaidia kazi, si kutumia nguvu zote zilizokusudiwa kwa kazi yenyewe.

Wakati kuanza kunapokuwa kugumu sana

Ikiwa hata kazi ndogo zimekuwa ngumu kuzifikia kwa zaidi ya wiki mbili, au umekuwa ukihisi kukata tamaa au kukosa hamasa isivyo kawaida, fikiria kuzungumza na mwanasaikolojia aliyeidhinishwa au daktari. Ugumu wa kuanza unaweza kuwa na sababu nyingi; huhitaji kuzitatua peke yako.

Ukiwa katika hatari ya haraka au ukifikiria kujidhuru, piga 999 au 112 nchini Kenya, au fungua ukurasa wa Dharura.
