When money is on your mind, give yourself some room

It is late. You have been checking the same figures again: rent, transport, food, data, a bill due soon. Perhaps you are waiting for a client to pay, working out how to stretch a small business float, or counting the days to payday. The details differ. The familiar part is doing the sums again, hoping the numbers might have become kinder.

Your body is ready for bed. Your mind is still at the table, checking the figures and looking for a way through.

“Just relax” is not much help when the bills are real. So let us leave the bills where they are for a moment and make a little room around the worry. Not to pretend the problem has disappeared. Just to give yourself a quieter place from which to meet it.

Why a money worry can take up so much space

Financial pressure rarely stays neatly inside a wallet. It can follow you into conversations, concentration and sleep. When an important problem keeps asking for attention, there may be less room for the other things you need to think about.

A 2013 study published in Science explored how financial pressure can affect thinking. In one experiment, people completed thinking tasks while considering how they would pay for a car repair. A large bill affected the performance of lower-income participants more than that of higher-income participants. In another study, sugarcane farmers in India performed better after harvest, when they had been paid, than before it. The researchers suggested that scarcity takes up mental bandwidth that would otherwise be available for other tasks.

The researchers compared the effect to losing a night of sleep. Later researchers have debated how large the effect really is, so that comparison is an illustration, not a literal measurement. The everyday point is easier to recognise: when a worry keeps returning, it can be harder to give other things your attention.

That is not a personal failing. It is a mind trying to keep an important concern in view.

A small pause, without pretending

You may not be able to bring in a client tonight, find a job by morning, increase a salary or settle every payment. But you can give yourself a short pause before deciding what comes next.

The World Health Organization’s free guide, Doing What Matters in Times of Stress, introduces practical skills such as grounding, stepping back from difficult thoughts, acting on your values and making room for uncomfortable feelings. The guide encourages brief, regular practice. The exercise below is inspired by grounding; it is not a financial solution or a replacement for professional support.

A quiet reset · two minutes

If your mind keeps reopening the same mental spreadsheet, try this without rushing.

  1. Notice where you are. Feel your feet on the floor, or your back supported by the mattress. Look around and name two ordinary things you can see.
  2. Let your breathing settle. Breathe comfortably. If it feels natural, let the out-breath be a little slower. There is no need to force a deep breath or count perfectly.
  3. Give the worry one clear sentence. For example: I need to find KSh ___ for rent, stock, transport or another expense by ___.
  4. Separate tonight from tomorrow. Ask whether there is one useful action you can take in the next 24 hours. If there is, write it down. If not, write Not tonight and leave the paper somewhere you can find it in the morning.

The bill has not vanished. But the worry now has a name, a possible next step and a place to wait until morning.

Give tomorrow a place to begin

Choose the moment when the calculations usually return:

If–then planIf I start doing money sums in bed, then I’ll write down the number and one possible action, and leave the note on the table until morning.

There is a related finding from a small sleep-lab study of 57 healthy adults aged 18 to 30. Participants who wrote down tasks they still needed to do fell asleep about nine minutes faster than those who wrote about tasks they had completed. It was a small study, not a guarantee. Still, putting tomorrow’s tasks on paper may make it easier to stop rehearsing them in your head.

When the worry needs more support

If money worries keep you awake most nights, bring on panic or leave you feeling hopeless, consider speaking with a licensed psychologist or doctor. Financial strain and low mood can overlap. You do not need to have your finances in order before you are allowed to ask for support.

If you are in immediate danger or thinking of ending your life, call 999 or 112 in Kenya, or open the Emergency page.

Wasiwasi wa pesa unapochukua nafasi, jipe nafasi kidogo

Ni usiku. Umekuwa ukikagua hesabu zilezile: kodi, nauli, chakula, data na bili inayokaribia. Labda unasubiri mteja akutumie malipo, unajaribu kupanga mtaji mdogo wa biashara, au unasubiri siku ya mshahara. Hali zinatofautiana. Kinachofanana ni kurudia hesabu, kana kwamba namba zinaweza kuwa zimebadilika na kuwa rafiki zaidi.

Mwili uko tayari kulala. Akili bado iko mezani, ikikagua hesabu na kutafuta njia ya kusonga mbele.

“Tulia tu” si ushauri unaosaidia sana wakati bili ni za kweli. Kwa hiyo tuziache pembeni kwa muda mfupi na tuupe wasiwasi nafasi kidogo. Si kwa kujifanya tatizo limetoweka. Ni kujipa mahali tulivu zaidi pa kuanzia kulikabili.

Kwa nini mawazo ya pesa yanaweza kuchukua nafasi kubwa

Shinikizo la kifedha haliishii kwenye pochi. Linaweza kufuatana nawe kwenye mazungumzo, umakini na usingizi. Tatizo muhimu linapoendelea kuomba umakini, nafasi ya kufikiria mambo mengine inaweza kupungua.

Utafiti uliochapishwa katika jarida la Science mwaka 2013 ulichunguza jinsi shinikizo la kifedha linavyoweza kuathiri kufikiri. Katika jaribio moja, watu walifanya kazi za kufikiri huku wakifikiria jinsi ya kulipia ukarabati wa gari. Gharama kubwa iliathiri zaidi matokeo ya washiriki wa kipato cha chini kuliko wale wa kipato cha juu. Katika utafiti mwingine, wakulima wa miwa nchini India walifanya vizuri zaidi baada ya mavuno, walipokuwa wamelipwa, kuliko kabla ya mavuno. Watafiti walipendekeza kuwa uhaba huchukua nafasi ya umakini ambao ungeweza kutumika kwa mambo mengine.

Watafiti walilinganisha athari hiyo na kupoteza usiku mmoja wa usingizi. Watafiti wa baadaye wamejadili ukubwa halisi wa athari hiyo; kwa hiyo ulinganisho huo ni mfano wa kueleza, si kipimo halisi. Jambo la kila siku ni rahisi kulitambua: wasiwasi unaporudi mara kwa mara, inaweza kuwa vigumu kuelekeza umakini kwenye mambo mengine.

Huo si udhaifu wa kibinafsi. Ni akili inayojaribu kuweka jambo muhimu mbele ya macho.

Pumziko dogo, bila kujifanya

Huenda usipate mteja usiku huu, kazi kesho asubuhi, nyongeza ya mshahara au fedha za kulipa kila kitu. Lakini unaweza kujipa nafasi fupi kabla ya kuamua hatua inayofuata.

Mwongozo wa bure wa Shirika la Afya Duniani, Doing What Matters in Times of Stress, unaeleza mbinu kama kujikita katika wakati uliopo, kujitenga kidogo na mawazo magumu, kutenda kulingana na thamani zako na kuyapa hisia ngumu nafasi. Mwongozo unapendekeza mazoezi mafupi ya mara kwa mara. Zoezi lifuatalo limechochewa na mbinu ya grounding; si suluhisho la kifedha wala mbadala wa msaada wa kitaalamu.

Pumziko tulivu · dakika mbili

Akili ikiendelea kufungua hesabu zilezile, jaribu haya bila haraka.

  1. Tambua ulipo. Sikia miguu yako sakafuni, au mgongo wako ukiegemezwa na godoro. Tazama chumba na utaje vitu viwili vya kawaida unavyoviona.
  2. Ruhusu pumzi itulie. Pumua kwa namna inayokufaa. Ikiwa ni rahisi, acha pumzi ya kutoka iwe ya taratibu kidogo. Huhitaji kujilazimisha kuvuta pumzi ndefu au kuhesabu kikamilifu.
  3. Eleza wasiwasi kwa sentensi moja. Kwa mfano: Nahitaji kupata KSh ___ za kodi, bidhaa, nauli au gharama nyingine kufikia ___.
  4. Tenganisha usiku wa leo na kesho. Jiulize kama kuna hatua moja ya maana unayoweza kuchukua ndani ya saa 24. Ikiwa ipo, iandike. Ikiwa haipo, andika Si usiku huu na uweke karatasi mahali utakapoiona asubuhi.

Bili haijatoweka. Lakini sasa wasiwasi una jina, hatua inayowezekana na mahali pa kusubiri hadi asubuhi.

Upe kesho mahali pa kuanzia

Chagua wakati ambao hesabu hizo hurudi mara nyingi:

Mpango wa ikiwa-basiNikianza kuhesabu pesa kitandani, basi nitaandika kiasi na hatua moja inayowezekana, kisha nitaacha karatasi mezani hadi asubuhi.

Kuna pia utafiti mdogo wa usingizi uliohusisha watu wazima 57 wenye afya, wenye umri wa miaka 18 hadi 30. Washiriki walioandika kazi walizohitaji kufanya walilala takriban dakika tisa haraka kuliko walioandika kazi walizokuwa wamemaliza. Utafiti ulikuwa mdogo; si ahadi ya matokeo hayo kwa kila mtu. Hata hivyo, kuandika kazi za kesho kunaweza kusaidia kuacha kuzirudia akilini.

Wakati wasiwasi unahitaji msaada zaidi

Ikiwa mawazo ya pesa yanakukosesha usingizi karibu kila usiku, yanaleta hofu kali au yanakuacha bila matumaini, fikiria kuzungumza na mwanasaikolojia aliyeidhinishwa au daktari. Shinikizo la kifedha na hali ya huzuni vinaweza kuingiliana. Huhitaji kwanza kupanga fedha zako ndipo uruhusiwe kuomba msaada.

Ukiwa katika hatari ya haraka au ukifikiria kujidhuru, piga 999 au 112 nchini Kenya, au fungua ukurasa wa Dharura.
